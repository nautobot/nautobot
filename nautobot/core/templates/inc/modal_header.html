{% comment %} `title_id` is the `id` that the enclosing `.modal` points its `aria-labelledby` at, so that the dialog takes its accessible name from this heading. It has to stay stable across HTMX swaps of the modal content, which is why it is passed in by the modal rather than derived from the title text. It defaults to the id used by `inc/generic_modal.html`. {% endcomment %}
